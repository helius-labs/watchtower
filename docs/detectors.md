# What it detects

| Detector                 | Signals                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `program-deployment`     | Upgrade, upgrade-authority transfer, program made immutable, program closed, loader-v4 migration, ProgramData resize. Each alert says whether it ran through a Squads multisig, by CPI, or by direct signature.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `upgrade-proposal`       | Early warning: an upgrade, authority change, close or migration of a watched program **proposed** in its Squads multisig, before it can execute. This includes large proposals that Squads writes through a transaction buffer first, which Watchtower reassembles from the buffer writes and checks against the hash Squads stores. Each alert states the approvals and time lock still standing in the way, and later approvals of that proposal alert too.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `multisig-proposal`      | Every other proposal on a watched multisig, decoded into what it would do: "transfer 5,000 SOL to …", "call `update_admin` on Drift (admin-only)". **Critical** when an approval or execution is signed against a durable nonce, the pattern reported in the Drift takeover. High for a proposal from a member who has not proposed before, and escalated when a proposal executes within an hour of being created.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `multisig-change`        | **Squads v4:** config proposals and executions (members, threshold, time lock, spending limits, rent collector), changes made by a config authority without a vote, spending-limit use. **Squads v3:** member and threshold changes. A threshold of 1 or a time lock of 0 is critical, and an execution inherits the severity of its proposal.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `mint-authority`         | Mint, freeze and Token-2022 authority changes and revocations; mints of at least 1% of supply; transfer hook, fee, pause, default account state, UI multiplier and interest rate changes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `permanent-delegate`     | A Token-2022 permanent delegate moving or burning tokens without the owner's signature. **Critical** with the **drain** stage when it takes tokens from a treasury or discovered vault, which `treasury-outflow` cannot see: Parsed Events names the signing delegate as the sender, and the owner's address is not in the transaction. High when the delegate of a watched mint moves or burns a holder's tokens, which is either the issuer seizing them or a stolen delegate key; that includes tokens the delegate holds itself, since a transaction does not say who owns the source account. PYUSD's delegate took 450,030 PYUSD from one holder on 2026-04-17, which is the test case.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `privileged-instruction` | Every call to an instruction in `privilegedInstructions`, classified by what it changes: **admin control** (`set_admin`, `transfer_authority`: high), **new markets or assets** (`initialize_spot_market`, `init_reserve`: high), **oracles** (high) and **risk settings** such as limits and weights (medium). A newly listed token is vetted: one created less than 30 days before the listing, or whose top 10 holders own 90% of supply, makes the alert critical. A signer not seen before is high, and a failed attempt by an unknown signer is high, since it often means someone is probing access control.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `admin-key-behavior`     | A known admin key used in a way its own history doesn't show, which is how a stolen key that already holds admin rights shows up: new-signer and wallet-age checks can't see it. Watchtower profiles each admin key's signed transactions on the program: the instructions it calls (by name, or by tag when undecoded), its fee payers and co-signers, and the other programs in those transactions. **Strong signals:** a call after a long quiet spell (at least 21 days, and at least 3× the key's longest earlier one), an instruction the key has never called, or a flash loan in the transaction. **Weak signals:** a new fee payer or co-signer (including the key paying its own fee for the first time), or a program never used alongside it. One strong signal is high, a strong signal plus any other is critical, two weak signals are medium and one is low. New instructions, parties and programs count once a key has 10 profiled transactions. A quiet spell counts from the start, so a cold key used a few times a year is covered: before its first signed call, the clock starts where Watchtower began watching it (the start of the lookback window, or of a replay window). A flash loan counts from the first transaction unless the key already uses that lending program. Replaying Raydium's December 2022 exploit, it raised a critical alert two hours before the first drain transaction in Raydium's own list: the owner key, normally co-signing with an operations wallet, signed again 25 days after its last call (its longest quiet spell before was 4 days), paid its own fee and called `withdraw_pnl` for the first time. Replaying Pump.fun's May 2024 exploit, it raised a critical alert on the attacker's first transaction (new fee payer, marginfi, flash loan), and nothing on the 474 routine withdraws before it. |
| `treasury-outflow`       | Net SOL and token outflows from `treasuries` and from discovered program vaults. One outflow at its threshold is high; 3× the threshold within the window is critical. Three or more assets leaving within the window is a critical **sweep**. With `allowedDestinations` set, any outflow elsewhere is high. Outflows are matched by the treasury's token accounts as well as its address, since a transfer signed by a delegate names the delegate as sender. One not signed by the treasury itself is a high **Outflow signed by a delegate**, with the delegate named; a permanent delegate's is left to `permanent-delegate`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `key-authority`          | Control handed off in a transaction signed by a treasury or watched key: stake staker or withdrawer authority (withdrawer is critical), stake lockup, token account owner (critical) or close authority, token delegate approvals (also on a vault's token account approved by its program through CPI, which only the live stream sees), and System `assign` of the account to a program (critical). Lists the other instructions in the same transaction, since the SwissBorg/Kiln exploit reportedly hid authority changes inside an unstake.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `nonce-authority`        | A durable nonce account created for, or reassigned to, a privileged key: an upgrade authority, admin key, multisig member, config authority, mint or freeze authority, known admin signer, or anything in `watchedKeys`. Anyone can do this for any key, and a transaction that key signs against the nonce never expires.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `idl-change`             | A newly published IDL for a watched program, listing instructions added and removed. High when a new instruction looks admin-only. `watch` checks every `idlPollMinutes`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `config-change`          | A changed field in a program's config accounts, such as Drift's `State` or Kamino's `GlobalConfig`, read through the IDL layout and compared every `configPollSeconds` (default 60) and right after any admin call. An admin key change is **critical** however it happened: a renamed instruction, a CPI, or one the classifier misses. Changed public keys, status and pause flags are high; limits, fees and collateral settings are medium. Counters and timestamps are ignored.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `build-verification`     | An upgrade whose bytecode still does not match a verified build 30 minutes later: critical when the program has registered source for verification, high when it has none. Every upgrade alert also says whether its bytecode is a verified build.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `attack-chain`           | One critical alert when a protocol shows two stages of a takeover within 24 hours: **control** (admin, authority or multisig changes, nonce-signed approvals, new admin signers), **risk** (listings, oracle or limit changes) and **drain** (large, sustained or swept outflows). Control plus risk alerts before any funds move, which is the window between a new admin's first calls and a drain: in the Drift exploit, the new admin listed a three-week-old token and raised withdrawal limits in its first transaction. It alerts again when a further stage appears.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

| Escalation    | Rule                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Durable nonce | Any medium-or-higher finding goes up one level when its transaction used a durable nonce, since it could have been signed long before it landed. |

Admin keys, config accounts, vaults and verified builds are found without configuration; see [discovery](discovery.md).

## How alerts stay complete

- **Cursors:** each watched address has one, stored in `.watchtower/state.json`. A cursor only moves past slots that have been scanned.
- **Live stream:** cursors advance to the latest notification's slot once every notification before it is processed. They stop the moment the connection drops, and while any subscription is rejected, so the next gap-fill covers the gap.
- **First scan:** each scan plan's first scan records history without alerting. After that, every new finding it scans alerts, including when another plan for the same program is new.
- **Scan budget:** each scan stops at `backfill.maxScannedTransactions`. A first scan reads newest-first, so on a busy address it covers only the most recent part of the lookback window and is marked **Recent only**; the older history is not scanned later. A later catch-up scan that stops at the budget is marked **Behind** and continues from the same place on the next run. Live monitoring sees every new transaction either way.
- **`report`:** findings from `report` are queued, and `watch` delivers them.
- **Failed deliveries:** they stay queued and are retried on the next start. Discord and Slack rate limits are retried automatically.
- **Duplicates:** findings are keyed by detector, signature and instruction position, so a transaction that arrives from both backfill and the stream alerts once.
- **Repeats:** when the same alert (same detector, subject and title) fires again within `alerts.groupWindowSeconds` (default 600) of the first, the first is sent at once and the rest arrive as one summary when the window closes, such as "Risk setting changed: update_withdraw_guard_threshold: 13 more", with their transactions listed. Critical alerts are always sent on their own. Every finding is still recorded and shown in the dashboard individually. Held repeats are sent when monitoring stops, and repeats among alerts left queued from an earlier run are combined the same way. Replaying the Drift exploit window, grouping cut the alerts sent on the day of the attack from 34 to 16 without delaying any critical one. Set `groupWindowSeconds: 0` to send every alert.
- **Live-only signals:** nonce setup for a privileged key has no history to backfill, because the key is only an instruction argument, not an account. `watch` subscribes to nonce instructions chain-wide and filters them locally, and `report` lists existing nonce accounts instead.
